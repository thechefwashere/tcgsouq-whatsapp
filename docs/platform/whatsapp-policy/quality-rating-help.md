# About your WhatsApp Business phone number’s quality rating

This article is intended for businesses that use [WhatsApp Business Platform](/help/325994829075700). If your business uses only the WhatsApp Business app, go to [this article](https://www.whatsapp.com/coronavirus/get-started-business) instead.

Your quality rating is a rating based on the recent messages that your customers have received over the past 7 days. This rating is determined by feedback from your customers like the reason they blocked your number and other reporting issues.

You can find the quality rating below the **Phone numbers** tab in your Meta WhatsApp Manager. The **Quality rating** column displays the quality states, including:

- **Green**: High quality
- **Yellow**: Medium quality
- **Red**: Low quality

You can become eligible for more features when your business maintains a **Green** quality rating. If your quality rating is **Yellow** or **Red**, you can hover over the rating in the WhatsApp Manager to view the insights into the block reasons if they're available.

When a user blocks your business, they can select a block reason:

- **No longer needed**
- **Didn’t sign up**
- **Spam**
- **Offensive messages**
- **No reason**

These block reasons indicate what you can do to improve low quality templates and mitigate the risk of future blocks to your phone number.

To find your quality rating over the past 30 days, click the icon in the **Settings** column. You can view the lowest quality score for your business on a given day, so you can see potential drivers that may have caused your quality rating to decline or increase.

**Note**: The phone number statuses of **Flagged** and **Restricted** have been discontinued as of Oct 7th, 2025.

## Message limits

[Message limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/api/rate-limits#messaging) determine the maximum number of unique WhatsApp user phone numbers your business portfolio can deliver messages to, outside of a [customer service window](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/guides/send-messages#customer-service-windows), within a moving 24-hour period. This limit is shared by all WhatsApp phone numbers in the business portfolio.

After you have completed one of the [three scaling paths](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/595597942906808), your message limits will be increased to 2000 per business portfolio. There are 4 business-initiated conversation limits you can reach after scaling:

- 2,000.
- 10,000.
- 100,000.
- Unlimited.

Your business portfolio will be upgraded to the next tier if:

- More than 50% of your current limit was used in the last 7 days.
- You are sending [high-quality messages](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/guides/send-messages#message-quality) across all your business phone numbers and templates.

Your limit will increase within 6 hours after these criteria are met.

Learn more [about best practices to enable more features and reach more customers.](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/595597942906808)
