# How to remove a partner from your WhatsApp Business account

Explore our [help content library](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/finding-information) to find answers and troubleshoot issues.

This article includes instructions for the desktop experience only.

You can remove a partner’s access to your WhatsApp Business account and its assets.

## Before you begin

- You need full control of the business portfolio that owns the WhatsApp Business account you want to remove the partner from
- You don’t need to first remove the partner’s access to your WhatsApp Business account or any other assets in order to remove them from those business assets.

## Remove a partner from your WhatsApp Business account

Start by going to [Settings](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings). Then follow the steps below:

1. Select the Meta Business account from the drop down menu in the top left.
2. Click on **Accounts**. Then, click on **WhatsApp Accounts**.
3. Select the WhatsApp Business account you’d like to remove the partner from.
4. Click on the **Partners** tab.
5. Click the icon that looks like a trash can next to the partner’s name.
6. Click **Confirm.**

These actions will remove the partner from the selected WhatsApp Business account. Follow the steps again above to remove them from other WhatsApp Business accounts.

**Note:** The partner may still have access to the owning Meta Business Account. Follow the steps in the section below to remove the partner from the Meta Business Account, if applicable.

## Remove a partner from your Meta Business Account

1. Select the business portfolio from the dropdown menu in the top left.
2. Click on **Users**. Then, click on **Partners**.
3. Select the partner you want to remove.
4. Click  **Options** in the top right and then click **Remove from business portfolio**.
5. Click **Remove partner** to confirm.

Once you have successfully removed a partner from your WhatsApp Business account and the business portfolio, they will no longer have the needed access to work on any of your business assets. Any data you share with a partner is subject to that partner’s data handling and privacy policies.

**Note:** The owning business portfolio will still be active after you remove the partner. Any assets owned by this portfolio like WhatsApp Business accounts, Pages, ad accounts and more, will remain connected to the portfolio in accordance with [Meta’s privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy).

Learn more about how to delete your [portfolio](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1592865014304024?id=180505742745347) if you no longer want to use it.
