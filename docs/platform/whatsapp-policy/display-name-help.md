# About WhatsApp Business display name

This article is intended for businesses that use [WhatsApp Business Platform](/business/help/325994829075700). Understand the [differences between the WhatsApp Business Platform and WhatsApp Business app](/business/help/338665938087159).

Explore our [help content library](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/finding-information) to find answers and troubleshoot issues.

The WhatsApp Business display name is your business name that customers see on your WhatsApp Business profile. When you add a new phone number to your WhatsApp Business account in your business portfolio, you assign a display name to it. The display name is used to create a certificate that is required to register a WhatsApp Business Platform account.

Display names should be related to your business and must not violate [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy). In addition, the display name must comply with our [display name guidelines](/business/help/757569725593362) to send messages using the WhatsApp Business Platform or to qualify for the WhatsApp official business account.

## WhatsApp Business display name review

You don't need to complete a WhatsApp display name review or account checks to get [started on WhatsApp Business Platform](/business/help/2640149499569241). You can begin sending messages to customers immediately. After sign-up:

- We’ll review the display name for consistency with the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy)
- Your requested display name becomes eligible for review once your business becomes eligible for higher messaging limits. Learn more about [how to find your business’s current messaging limit in WhatsApp Manager and how to increase it](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/messaging-limits/).
- You’ll get a notification to download the certificate immediately after your requested WhatsApp Business display name is approved. Note that only On-Premises API businesses and business service providers representing On-Premises businesses need to download the certificate. Remember that the certificate for the approved display name is only available for 14 days. If the certificate expires, you'll need to follow the steps above to submit the display name for review again.

**Note:** Any changes made to the display name after initiating a display name review will need to be reviewed and approved before the updated display name can be used.

## WhatsApp official business account

If your business wants to have an WhatsApp official business account, it must be considered a notable business. A notable business is a well-known business that has a substantial presence in news articles from publications with sizable audiences. WhatsApp official business accounts are issued for phone numbers. With the same phone number, you can use the current or a new business name to apply for the WhatsApp official business account. Learn more [about how to request a WhatsApp official business account](/business/help/604726921052590).
